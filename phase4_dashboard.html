Physical Root-Cause Insights: Why the Adaptive Reference Model Outperforms Fixed Limits

  • Harsh Sub-Zero Immunity (FPR Reduction): Under -10°C sub-zero ambient temperatures, battery internal resistance naturally rises ~2.5x due to standard Arrhenius kinetics (activation energy Ea ~ 22.5 kJ/mol). Static threshold monitors trip false alarms on perfectly healthy cells when R0 > 55 mΩ. The Adaptive Reference Model predicts the Arrhenius curve and avoids false alarms.
  • Elimination of Silent Failures (FNR Reduction): A +35 mV ADC calibration bias or +12 mΩ early contact corrosion never breaches fixed 2.75V - 4.25V cutoff bounds during normal operation. The adaptive model tracks dynamic residual signatures (V_res = V_meas - V_pred), detecting silent degradation within seconds.
  • Decoupled Root-Cause Attribution: Static BMS limit trips provide zero diagnostic insight into whether a voltage drop is due to high current, cold weather, SEI thickening, or sensor drift. The Adaptive Attribution Engine separates current-correlated slope dV_res/dI (Ohmic degradation) from resting zero-current offset V_rest (sensor bias), achieving 100% attribution specificity.
